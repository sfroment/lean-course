Big picture

ConceptMeaning
termAny expression, e.g. 2 + 2, rfl
typeThe "kind" of a term. Every term has exactly one type
NatType of natural numbers: 0, 1, 2, …
PropType of propositions, e.g. 2 + 2 = 4, True
propositionA term of type Prop — a statement that may be true
proofA term whose type is the proposition. No separate notion!
kernelLean's small trusted core; re-checks every proof mechanically
sorryEscape hatch that admits a proof — always leaves a warning, never silent
