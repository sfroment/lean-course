# my-data/

Drop your exported `lean-answers-*.json` files here (or paste them in chat).
They contain: quiz answers with correctness, lessons marked done, and your
pencil-scratchpad Lean code.

The teacher (pi session) reads them from here, reviews your errors, runs your
Lean for real in `playground/` (`lake env lean <file>`), and writes review
notes under `reviews/`.
